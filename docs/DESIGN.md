## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a bespoke, high-end atmosphere tailored for artisan-crafted darts equipment, custom laser engraving, and tournament-grade accessories. The aesthetic bridges the gap between precision tournament craftsmanship and old-world luxury atelier aesthetics. 

The visual style blends **Dark Luxury Minimalism** with **Tactile Precision Details**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian surfaces, champagne and polished metallic gold accents, razor-sharp vector lines, and warm metallic specular reflections.
- **Target Audience:** Dedicated darts players, league competitors, collectors, and gift-buyers seeking personalized, tournament-ready darts shafts, flights, and cases.
- **Emotional Response:** Uncompromising precision, prestige, mastery, confidence, and bespoke artistry. Every surface should feel like hand-finished anodized metal or matte carbon.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure obsidian depths, nuanced charcoal surface elevations, and dynamic metallic gold highlights.

- **Background & Base Neutrals:**
  - `canvas`: `#0B0B0B` (pure dark obsidian backdrop)
  - `surface-1`: `#141414` (card and component backings)
  - `surface-2`: `#1E1E1E` (elevated states, dropdowns, modal windows)
  - `surface-border`: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` (subtle gold micro-hairlines)
  
- **Gold Spectrum (Accents & Highlights):**
  - `primary`: `#D4AF37` (classic bullion gold; active states, primary CTA, badges)
  - `secondary`: `#F3E5AB` (champagne tint; high-contrast focus rings, hovered metallic glows, delicate labels)
  - `tertiary`: `#AA7C11` (burnished dark gold; borders, pressed states, subtle gradients)

- **Typography & High Contrast Elements:**
  - `text-primary`: `#FFFFFF` (crisp, uncompromising readability)
  - `text-secondary`: `#E2E2E2` (supportive descriptions and metadata)
  - `text-muted`: `#8E8E93` (technical specs, timestamps, subtle captions)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes haute-couture editorial flair with technical engraving precision:

- **Display & Section Titles (Bodoni Moda):** Provides high-contrast, razor-thin serifs that immediately convey heirloom quality and personalized craftsmanship.
- **Editorial & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances geometric clarity with warm, approachable legibility against pitch-black backgrounds, preventing visual fatigue during configuration and reading.
- **Micro-Data & Engraving Coordinates (Space Grotesk):** Applied in uppercase tracking for technical dimensions, flight microns, barrel weights (e.g., `24G 90% TUNGSTEN`), SKU indicators, and live engraving preview labels.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column layout on desktop converts smoothly into a 6-column grid for tablets and a single-column stacked format on mobile devices.

- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Spacing values use a strict 4px/8px incremental base. High-ticket items must be granted expansive breathing room; hero showcases and custom engraving modules utilize generous vertical padding (`space-xl` and higher) to amplify an aura of rarity and exclusivity.
- **Grid Alignment:** Components, toolbars, and flight preview canvases adhere strictly to clean gutters, anchoring the eye along defined architectural axes.
- **Adaptive Reflow:** Interactive 3D/2D live flight personalizers anchor sticky controls to the bottom viewport on mobile, while desktop views leverage a split 7:5 ratio (7 cols for live visualizer, 5 cols for typography/monogram entry).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows. Instead, it defines hierarchy through **Tonal Stacking**, **Fine Metallic Borders**, and **Ambient Gold Haloes**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Pure `#0B0B0B`, unembellished, absorbing ambient light.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards, Panels):** Layered at `#141414` with a 1px boundary of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- **Floating Overlays (Modals, Customizer Palettes):** `#1E1E1E` with a hairline stroke of `rgba(243, 229, 171, 0.35)` accompanied by a subtle tinted bloom: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Focus & Selection Glow:** Interactive elements when active or hovered cast an understated champagne specular rim light: `0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is crisp and architectural (`Soft`, level 1), evoking machined edges, precision flight contours, and beveled display cases:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to buttons, input fields, badges, and segmented toggles.
- **Container Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to product cards, engraving preview boards, and modal dialogs.
- **Special Elements (Diamond & Bevel Accents):** Selected badges and iconography utilize 45-degree angled clipped corners or micro-chamfers to mirror dart barrel knurling and flight aerodynamics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Bespoke Gold Action):** Background in solid `#D4AF37`, text in `#0B0B0B` (bold `Space Grotesk`), subtle linear top sheen (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`). On hover, shifts to `#F3E5AB` with a 12px soft gold bloom.
- **Secondary (Dark Machined):** Background `#141414`, text in `#FFFFFF`, with a 1px border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`. Hover elevates border to `#D4AF37`.
- **Ghost / Text:** Uppercase `Space Grotesk` with an animated gold underline expanding from center.

### Interactive Customization Inputs
- **Text & Monogram Input:** Matte `#141414` background, crisp 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text in pure `#FFFFFF`. Active focus transitions the border to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow. Micro-labels sit outside the input in gold tracking.
- **Engraving Depth / Finish Selectors:** Radio switches styled as brushed metal medallions; selected state is highlighted by an inner golden bullseye ring.

### Cards & Showcase Panels
- **Flight & Accessory Cards:** Solid `#141414` background surrounded by a 1px `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)` border. On hover, the border brightens to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)` while the product image scales smoothly (1.03x) under a subtle champagne spotlight gradient.
- **Meta Badges:** Minimalist micro-chips with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)` fill, `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)` border, and `#F3E5AB` mono text.

### Interactive Engraving Visualizer Component
- A dedicated split canvas featuring a real-time vector flight simulator. Dark radial vignette backdrop (`#1A1A1A` down to `#0B0B0B`) showing laser-engraving depth simulation via dynamic contrast shading and subtle gold foil reflections.
